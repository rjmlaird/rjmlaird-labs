A resistor, a filament lamp, and a diode all respond to potential difference differently. Sweep the applied p.d. and see why only the resistor gives a straight line — the practical behind "some resistors keep a constant R, others don't."

Static Electricity & Electric Fields

Rubbing two insulators transfers electrons, leaving one positively and one negatively charged. GCSE asks for the qualitative picture: field strongest close to a charge, weaker further away, and a stronger force the closer two charges get. The exact force law (Coulomb's law) below is an extension, not required at GCSE.
