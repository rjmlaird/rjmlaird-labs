Interactive portfolio-strategy prototype

See where each offer sits before deciding where to invest.

Plot products, brands or business units against market growth and relative market share. The matrix can make portfolio conversations more concrete: which offers may deserve investment, which can fund development, which need a deliberate decision and which may need repositioning, harvesting or exit.

!
Use the BCG matrix as a prompt, not an automatic strategy. Share and growth are imperfect proxies for cash generation, attractiveness and strategic value. Consider profitability, customer need, capability, risk, competitive dynamics, mission, switching costs and future scenarios—especially in smaller or digital businesses.

Using the growth-share matrix well

    This prototype is a strategic-planning aid, not investment advice. Portfolio decisions affect customers, staff, partners and communities. Test assumptions with evidence, consider profitability, capability and risk beyond two axes, and validate material commitments before acting.
