Interactive startup-metrics prototype

Measure the question that matters right now.

Lean Analytics helps teams progress from a problem hypothesis to a scalable business by focusing on one core uncertainty at a time: customer need, retention, organic growth, sustainable revenue and efficient scale. Use each stage to define an experiment, select practical evidence and decide what must be true before moving forward.

!
Do not treat the stages as a universal, rigid sequence. A product may need to revisit earlier assumptions, and not every business relies on virality. The useful principle is to resolve the most consequential uncertainty before spending heavily on the next growth lever.

Experiment discipline

    This prototype supports structured learning and product planning. It does not guarantee product-market fit, investment outcomes or commercial success. Choose measures that respect privacy, avoid manipulative design, account for qualitative evidence and are suitable for your business model and customers.
