Hubble tuning fork

Ellipticals make the handle; unbarred and barred spirals form the upper and lower branches. Irregulars sit beyond this simplified diagram because they do not follow its regular structure.

Elliptical to S0

Ellipticals are smooth, arm-free systems. Lenticulars retain a disc and prominent bulge but lack obvious spiral arms.

Spiral sequence

From Sa to Sc, the illustrative bulge shrinks and the arms open. “Early” and “late” are morphological labels, not an evolutionary timetable.

Barred branch

Barred spirals have an elongated stellar bar; their arms emerge from its ends rather than directly around the central bulge.
