Interactive perceptual-mapping prototype

See the market through your audience’s eyes.

Map how customers perceive your brand and the alternatives they genuinely consider. Use two attributes that matter to purchase decisions—such as affordability and specialist expertise, or simplicity and capability—to reveal crowded positions, meaningful differentiation and potential opportunities to investigate.

!
An empty space is a research question, not automatically an opportunity. A blank area can signal unmet demand, but it can also reflect a trade-off customers do not value, a proposition that is difficult to deliver or missing competitors. Ground map positions in customer evidence and test any positioning hypothesis before investing.

How to use a positioning map

    This prototype is for strategic discussion and research planning. It does not establish objective product quality, market demand or competitor performance. Avoid unsupported comparative claims, misleading ratings, fabricated customer evidence and the collection of user-testing or survey data without appropriate consent and privacy safeguards.
