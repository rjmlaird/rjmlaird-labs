Visual stakeholder-mapping prototype

See who matters, how relationships work, and where engagement is needed.

Place a research finding at the centre of a visual constellation. Map stakeholder groups by influence, expected benefit, involvement, relationship strength, engagement need and exclusion risk—then identify where to listen, collaborate or build trust first.

!
Use this as a planning prompt, not a complete stakeholder analysis. Scores are subjective and should be discussed with relevant partners and communities. Do not use the tool to justify excluding groups from decisions that affect them.
