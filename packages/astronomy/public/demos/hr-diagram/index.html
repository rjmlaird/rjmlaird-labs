HR diagram explorer

Stars plotted by surface temperature and luminosity. Circle size is the star's radius, worked out from the other two, and colour follows temperature. Hover or click a star, or add your own.

Hot stars are on the left, as is tradition. Along any dashed line the radius is the same, so a star above and to the right of another is bigger. Luminosity is in solar units and absolute magnitude is shown on the right.
