Plan the message people need at each decision moment.

AIDA helps you structure campaign communications around four practical goals: become known, earn attention, make the proposition meaningful and help people take the next suitable step. Add Retention when ongoing customer value, trust, loyalty or advocacy matters after the initial conversion.

!
AIDA is a communication lens, not a literal customer journey. People compare alternatives, consult peers, revisit information and enter at different stages. Pair this canvas with customer research, channel data and accessible, truthful communications rather than assuming a single linear funnel.

Real-world AIDA tips

    This prototype supports marketing-communications planning. It does not predict individual purchase decisions or campaign outcomes. Use clear claims, appropriate consent, accessible formats and transparent measures; do not use deceptive scarcity, misleading proof, dark-pattern conversion tactics or privacy-invasive tracking.
