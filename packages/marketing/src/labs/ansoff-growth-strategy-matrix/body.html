Interactive Ansoff-matrix prototype

Explore growth beyond doing more of the same.

The Ansoff matrix structures growth choices around two questions: are you working with existing or new products, and existing or new markets? Use it to generate and evaluate strategic options across penetration, market development, product development and diversification.

!
Risk generally increases as unfamiliarity increases—but the matrix is not a risk score. Assess customer demand, strategic fit, capability, economics, delivery capacity, competition, regulation and ethical implications before committing resources. “New” must be defined for your specific organisation and target market.

Using the Ansoff matrix well

    This prototype is a strategic-planning tool, not investment advice. Growth initiatives can affect customers, communities, staff, suppliers and markets in different ways. Validate demand, accessibility, security, privacy, sustainability, regulatory duties, operational capability and financial resilience before making material commitments.
