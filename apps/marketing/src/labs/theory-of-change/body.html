Visual programme-design prototype

Make the route from resources to change visible.

Build a connected pathway from inputs and activities to outputs, near-term outcomes, longer-term outcomes and intended impact. Use assumptions, risks and evidence signals to turn an ambitious funding narrative into a more testable, evaluable programme design.

!
A theory of change is a hypothesis, not a guarantee. It should state what needs to be true, who needs to be involved, what could disrupt the pathway and how progress will be observed and adapted over time.
