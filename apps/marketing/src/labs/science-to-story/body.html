Science communication prototype

Keep the evidence. Change the route into it.

Turn technical source material into audience-aware communication drafts without losing the facts, caveats or limits that make the work trustworthy. Use the output as a structured first draft, then complete expert and editorial review before publishing.

!
Human review is required. This tool does not verify science, assess evidence quality, identify missing context, or approve claims. It only structures the facts, caveats and links that you provide into communication-ready draft material.
