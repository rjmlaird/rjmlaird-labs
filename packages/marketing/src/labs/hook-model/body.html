Interactive product-behaviour prototype

Design return behaviour around meaningful value.

Map the four parts of a repeat-use loop: what prompts a user, the easiest useful action, the value they receive and the contribution that makes the product more valuable next time. Use the model to reduce friction and support beneficial habits—not to exploit attention, anxiety or vulnerability.

!
Habit-forming is not automatically user-centred. Keep the user’s long-term interest as the design constraint. Avoid dark patterns, deceptive urgency, excessive notifications, unwanted data extraction and reward systems that encourage harmful or compulsive use.
