Plan acquisition around the value of the whole customer relationship.

Customer Lifetime Value estimates the present value of future contribution from a customer relationship. Use it to examine whether acquisition, onboarding and retention investments can be justified over time—not simply by the first transaction.

!
CLV is an estimate, not a promise or a licence to overspend. Base assumptions on cohorts and representative data, separate revenue from contribution margin, include service and retention costs where material, and update forecasts when retention, pricing, market conditions or customer behaviour changes.

CLV assumptions

This calculator projects expected contribution over a fixed horizon, applies annual retention and discounts future value to present value. Enter averages for a defined customer segment or cohort rather than blending incomparable customer types.

Enter average revenue per transaction or billing period.

For subscriptions, use billing periods per year.

Use a contribution-oriented margin where possible.

Retention is 100% minus annual churn rate.

Include attributable media, sales and onboarding costs.

Include support, success and retention costs where material.

Discounts future contribution to today’s value.

A longer horizon increases forecast uncertainty.

Use CLV responsibly

    This calculator is for scenario planning, not financial, tax or investment advice. Results depend entirely on the assumptions entered. Review historical cohorts, attribution, refunds, cost-to-serve, working capital, taxes, capacity, concentration risk and data quality with appropriate commercial and financial expertise before making decisions.
