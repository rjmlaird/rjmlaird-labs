Interactive marketing-planning prototype

Design the complete customer-facing offer.

Use the 7Ps to plan the product or service, price, place, promotion, people, process and physical evidence that shape a customer’s experience. It is a structured way to ensure marketing promises align with how the offer is actually delivered.

!
The seven Ps are interdependent. A persuasive campaign cannot compensate for an unclear offer, a difficult buying process or weak evidence of credibility. Review the mix together whenever customer needs, market conditions or the service design changes.
