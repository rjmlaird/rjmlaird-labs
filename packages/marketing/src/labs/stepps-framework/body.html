Interactive shareability-planning prototype

Give people a good reason to pass your idea on.

STEPPS helps you design content, products and campaigns that people may find useful, meaningful, visible and worth talking about. Map each principle around a clear audience need, then test the result with real people rather than assuming attention equals value.

!
Sharing should be voluntary and informed. Do not use misinformation, manufactured outrage, hidden incentives, deceptive scarcity or personal-data exploitation to create reach. A strong campaign earns attention by being relevant, truthful, accessible and genuinely useful.

Shareability with integrity

    This prototype supports communications planning. It does not guarantee reach, engagement or commercial results. Avoid misinformation, harmful stereotyping, manufactured outrage, deceptive incentives and inaccessible formats. Build campaigns around consent, evidence, accessibility and value for the communities you aim to reach.
