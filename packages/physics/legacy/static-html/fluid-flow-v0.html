Flow around objects

Watch a simple visual model of fluid moving around an aerofoil or square. Change the medium and speed to see how the flow pattern responds.

Aerofoil in air Flow speed: 4 · Visual model
Flow particles Object and wake

This is an illustrative simulation rather than a computational-fluid-dynamics solver. The particles are deflected around the selected shape, and a wake is added behind it to make separation and turbulence visible. A full CFD model would calculate pressure and velocity by solving the governing equations on a mesh.
