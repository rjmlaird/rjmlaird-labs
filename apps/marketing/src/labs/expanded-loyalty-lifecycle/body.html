Expanded lifecycle marketing planner

Turn first contact into a relationship—not just a conversion.

Customer lifecycle marketing coordinates relevant communications, service and customer experiences from discovery through repeat value and voluntary advocacy. This planner combines the loyalty ladder with the RACE lifecycle—Reach, Act, Convert and Engage—to reveal gaps across the journey rather than treating marketing as a series of isolated campaigns.

!
Personalisation should be useful, proportionate and controllable. Use customer information to reduce friction and increase relevance—not to exploit vulnerability, create false urgency, conceal choices or make disengagement difficult. Customers may pause, lapse and return; meaningful re-engagement starts by restoring value and trust.

Lifecycle marketing principles

    This lifecycle planner supports marketing and customer-experience discussions; it is not legal, financial or privacy advice. Use behavioural, CRM, analytics and advertising data lawfully and transparently. Minimise data collection, protect preference controls, accommodate accessibility needs and avoid practices that manipulate, exclude or obstruct customers.
