Understand the forces shaping your market.

Assess competitive rivalry, buyer power, supplier power, the threat of new entrants and the threat of substitutes. Use the map to identify where pressure sits, what makes it stronger, and how your organisation could be less exposed.

!
Use this for industry structure, not a generic competitor list. The model asks how value is distributed among industry actors and what makes a market more or less attractive—not simply which brands look similar today.

Set up the analysis

Define a specific market, then score each competitive force from 1 (weak pressure) to 5 (very strong pressure). Add a concise rationale for the force you are reviewing.

Keep the definition focused: product/service, customer group and geography where relevant.
